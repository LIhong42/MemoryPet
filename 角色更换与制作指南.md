## AI制作角色教程

## AI 生图提示词模板

下面的模板适合拿去生成角色素材。

### 角色设定图

```text
Create an original chibi anime desktop pet character design. The entire canvas must have an exact 3:4 width-to-height aspect ratio.
Style: clean Japanese anime illustration, cute but polished, no pixel art.
Character: [写角色外貌，例如 silver white hair, black ribbons, red eyes, elegant school-style outfit].
Personality: [写性格，例如 proud but caring, slightly tsundere, refined and calm].
Requirements: full body, front-facing, consistent proportions, clear silhouette, simple readable details, transparent or plain white background, no text, no watermark.
Avoid: inconsistent costume, extra accessories, cropped feet, blurry image, realistic photo style, heavy shadows.
```
### 动作生成
```text
Create a desktop pet animation storyboard based on the uploaded character reference image.

- Automatically determine the number of frames based on action complexity, using no more than 6 frames.

- Prioritize smooth, continuous motion. Each frame must be a small, natural progression from the previous frame, with no sudden pose changes, large displacements, or abrupt movements.

- Design lightweight, cute, agile movements that suit the character. Coordinate body movements with natural secondary motion in the hair and clothing.

- Make each frame visibly distinct while keeping adjacent frames sufficiently similar for smooth animation playback. Every frame has to involve coordinated changes in facial expressions, hand movements, and leg movements.

- Transparent background. No text, labels, props, or watermark.

Design only the following action. Do not introduce unrelated actions.

## Action Overview
动作描述
```

### 默认动作表


1. **Idle** Subtle breathing, blinking, and gentle hair/clothing movement; return naturally to the initial pose. 
2. **RunningRight — 8 frames:** Smooth running cycle facing right, with alternating limbs, airborne phases, and landing. Last 2 cells empty.
3. **RunningLeft — 8 frames:** Same running cycle facing left, with natural, mirrored movement. Last 2 cells empty.
4. **WalkingRight — 8 frames:** Relaxed walking cycle facing right, with alternating steps and opposite arm swings; slower and less bouncy than running. Last 2 cells empty.
5. **WalkingLeft — 8 frames:** Relaxed walking cycle facing left, with smooth steps and natural arm movement. Last 2 cells empty.
6. **Waving — 10 frames:** Frames 1–5 show a complete left-hand wave; frames 6–10 show a complete right-hand wave. Include raising, waving, and lowering each hand.
7. **Jumping — 8 frames:** Crouch, takeoff, ascent, joyful apex, descent, landing, recovery, and return to standing. Last 2 cells empty.
8. **Failed — 6 frames:** Hesitation, surprise, embarrassment, frustration, dejection, and a cute troubled expression. Last 4 cells empty.
9. **HeartGesture — 10 frames:** Raise both hands, gradually form a recognizable heart, hold the pose with a sweet blush or smile, then gently return toward the starting pose.
10. **Dancing — 10 frames:** A lively, cute dance with alternating sways, varied arm positions, playful steps or toe taps, and a small turn or bounce. End in a pose that connects naturally to the first frame.
